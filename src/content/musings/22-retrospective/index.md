I'm 23 today. Happy one year closer to death[^oneyearcloser] for me!

This is the second retrospective post I'm making for my birthday. Again, still
cliche considering no one reads this shit, but there's a lot to talk about. I
like to think I'm an interesting enough person.

If you're interested, visit [Year of Varun #21](/musings/21-retrospective) for
the past year's edition of this post.

## Jobs

Somehow I cycled through getting three jobs in the same year. Call it the
pinnacle of inconsistency, or maybe call it a flex because
[getting interviews in this job market is a complete crapshoot](https://www.businessinsider.com/big-tech-job-market-hiring-cracked-layoffs-amazon-microsoft-2025-12).
But hey, gotta start somewhere right?

First, my lucky stint with [ANDYL](https://andyl.com), where I met the CEO at a
Nix user group at UC Berkeley. Afterward, I had another stint with
[Pangolin](https://pangolin.net) for a while. And now, I'm at
[Neuralink](https://neuralink.net), offer in hand, starting next week. I'm super
thankful for everyone that gave me opportunities to grow in my own technical
skills (and other skills too!), while teaching me some pretty hard lessons along
the way, and I'm also super excited for the new opportunities coming my way at
Neuralink[^n7k].

Moving between jobs like this, however, is taxing. Pop tech culture dictates
that employees are only
[a net positive on productivity after 6 months](https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions/comments/wstgmt/in_what_worlds_is_6_months_to_become_productive/);
however true/untrue this may be, the reality is that _onboarding is hard_, and
becoming a net productivity gain is only harder.

So, my takeaway for this year, based on all this movement? **Stay put at this
new job for as long as possible.** This is so I don't hamper my own growth, and
so I can start to value stability as much as I say I do. Actions speak louder
than words, after all. Plus, I really like the people on my team already. They
all are really cool people.

## Open Source

I am still continuing my open-source projects. In fact, I moved a lot of them to
[SourceHut](https://sourcehut.org/), and I'm glad I did, considering the massive
amounts of
[GitHub outages](https://damrnelson.github.io/github-historical-uptime/) that
have been happening[^github-outages].

Working on them concurrently with work has been a bit of a challenge recently; I
noticed that I tend to work on these projects in bursts. Since I'm often the
sole maintainer, I have to moderate this kind of work though, because burning
out on FOSS work is just not the way I want to go out.

## More School??

Remember how last year I thought 2025 was gonna be my last year of school?

Turns out that may not be the case.

I got accepted into Georgia Tech right around the time I received a job offer
from Neuralink! So I'm gonna have to figure out how to balance an online
cybersecurity degree with the full-time work I'm doing.

Funny enough, I decided to do this program on impulse, but I think it will end
up doing me a whole ton of good. I want to teach in the future, and getting a
masters is a surefire way of ensuring I can do that down the line.

## Music

Unfortunately, this year was not a good year for my music career. I've just been
so goddamn busy that I've been neglecting practice most of the time.

I don't think this is a priority shift as much as it is just me being lazy.
After I stopped playing for churches, I just didn't have a fire lit underneath
me to actually practice, so being the naturally lazy person I am, I didn't. And
my skills have definitely regressed. My musical intuition is still alive, but I
need to make sure I don't keep neglecting this or this will get worse.

Here's to practicing at night, in open fields, with a joint and sticks in hand.

On the other hand, I'm making music with Logic Pro a lot more than I used to.
It's therapeutic for me and I get to sample real-life noises and stuff with a
shitty room mic. Not to mention, with all the new music I've listened to in the
past year, I'm taking influence from genres and artists that high school me
would have disapproved of.

## What's Next?

Well, my resolutions for the year are quite clear. I'm a full-blown adult now
and I just need to make sure I'm living my dreams as needed. So, in order:

1. Stay consistent at work and keep innovating there.
2. Keep practicing music actively.
3. Go to the damn gym!
